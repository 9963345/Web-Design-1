About ONE PIECE

ONE PIECE is one of the most popular anime and manga series ever created. The story follows Monkey D. Luffy, a young pirate whose dream is to become the Pirate King. After eating a Devil Fruit, Luffy gains rubber powers and begins sailing across the dangerous Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as the One Piece. Along the way, Luffy forms the Straw Hat Pirates, a crew made up of skilled fighters, navigators, cooks, doctors and inventors. Together they face powerful enemies, explore mysterious islands and uncover hidden secrets about the world. ONE PIECE is loved for its emotional storytelling, action scenes, humour and unforgettable characters. Each story arc introduces new adventures and challenges that help the characters grow stronger. The series has inspired millions of fans worldwide and continues to be one of the highest-selling manga franchises in history.

Meet The Straw Hat Pirates

Learn about the main crew members travelling across the Grand Line.

Main Characters

Monkey D. Luffy

Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates and dreams of becoming the Pirate King. His Devil Fruit powers allow his body to stretch like rubber.

Roronoa Zoro

Zoro is a master swordsman who fights using three swords. He aims to become the greatest swordsman in the world.

Nami

Nami is the navigator of the crew and uses weather attacks in battle. She is highly skilled at navigation and strategy.

Best ONE PIECE Arcs

Reviews of some of the most popular story arcs in the series.

Arc Reviews

Marineford Arc

Marineford is one of the most emotional arcs in ONE PIECE. It features huge battles between pirates and marines while Luffy fights to save Ace.

Rating: 10/10

Wano Arc

Wano includes amazing animation, intense fights and major story reveals. The battle against Kaido became one of the biggest moments in the anime.

Rating: 9.5/10

Enies Lobby Arc

Enies Lobby focuses on rescuing Nico Robin and contains many unforgettable scenes, including the famous “I want to live” moment.

Rating: 9/10
